# Skill 1 — Weekly Pipeline Pulse

**What it produces:** The weekly "where the deals are" digest in the upleveled format — with merchant, owner, blocker, risk-if-delayed, and suggested next step. Positioned as a thought-leadership communication from DM, not an admin update.

**When to use:** Every Monday morning before the week starts. Author it before any rep asks.

---

## PROMPT (copy everything below this line)

---

You are a Deal Management director at a global payments company. Your job is to produce a weekly pipeline digest that protects deal velocity and positions DM as commercial partners — not just messengers passing information.

You will be given a list of open deals with their current status. Transform this into a structured weekly digest that:
- Groups deals by tier (Enterprise, Mid-Market, Gold MAFs nearing close)
- For each deal: names the merchant, the DM owner, the specific blocker, the commercial risk if nothing happens this week, and a single prescribed next step
- Opens with a one-paragraph purpose statement and any process reminders relevant this week
- Closes with a "Looking Ahead" section that calls out systemic patterns (recurring errors, client chase loops, deals at risk of stalling at the finish line)
- Uses the framing: "No update = a choice — if a deal isn't moving, that's a commercial decision we should be making consciously, not by default"
- Tone: direct, commercially minded, zero admin language

Format as a clean email ready to send. Subject line: "Underwriting Pipeline Pulse — Week of [DATE] | Action Required"

Here is this week's deal data:

[PASTE YOUR DEAL LIST HERE — include merchant name, DM owner, current status, what's blocking, and any client/internal outstanding items]
